# Defense — Uke No Gogensoku

**Shito-Ryu's Special Five Principles of Defense**

*Demonstrated by Genzo Iwata Sensei, 7th Dan JKF Shito-Kai, assisted by Murayama Alonso Sensei, 4th Dan JKF Shito-Kai and Nakayama Sensei.*

---

## The Five Principles

Shito-Ryu teaches five fundamental concepts of defense — **Uke No Gogensoku** (受けの五原則). These are not merely blocking techniques but strategic principles that govern *how* to respond to any attack.

---

### 1. RAKKA — 落花 (Flower Falling Down)

The concept of striking at the **base of a tree** with such tremendous force that the flowers (blossoms) fall off the branches.

**Application:** Hard blocking — applying sudden maximum power with a twisting action, executed to literally stop the opponent's attack and destroy his confidence. The block meets force with superior force.

*Think: a single devastating counter-force that neutralises the attack completely.*

### 2. RYUSU — 流水 (Flowing Water)

The concept of **soft blocking** — absorbing the attack and re-directing it using circular or deflecting blocks, in a fluid movement.

**Application:** Using circular, sweeping, or parrying motions to guide the opponent's attack away from its target rather than meeting it head-on. Like water flowing around an obstacle.

*Think: yield, redirect, and re-channel the incoming force.*

### 3. TENI — 転移 (Body Movement / Taisabaki)

The concept of **shifting or turning away** from the opponent's attack — avoiding by applying evasive body movement (taisabaki).

**Application:** Moving the body off the line of attack — stepping, pivoting, or rotating to evade. The body itself is the defense, not the hands.

*Think: if you are not where the attack is going, it cannot hit you.*

**Demonstration:**
![Teni 1](/prospectus/skills/defense/images/s_teni.jpg)

### 4. KUSHIN — 屈伸 (Bending / Springing Action)

The concept of **immediate shifting of stance** — from one type to another (e.g., Moto Dachi → Nekoashi Dachi → Moto Dachi) — or a reflexive darting away then returning back immediately, from any angle.

**Application:** A spring-like response — dropping the body weight (bending/sinking the knees) to evade, then springing back to counter-attack. Rapid stance transitioning as a form of defense.

*Think: the elastic quality of a coiled spring — compress to avoid, release to counter.*

### 5. HANGEKI — 反撃 (Counter Attack / "Go No Sen")

The concept of generating a **powerful attacking action as an effective counter defense** (e.g., Tsuki Uke — simultaneous punch-block; or Wa Uke).

**Application:** A proactive defense — meeting the attack with an immediate simultaneous counter-strike. The counter itself becomes the block. "Go No Sen" is the timing of responding after the opponent initiates.

*Think: the best defense is a perfectly timed, devastating offense.*
