title: Defense
---

High-level defensive ideas that describe how to absorb, redirect, and break incoming attacks.

## Reference Notes

Stances Movement Defense Blocks Punches Strikes Kicks Kata Kumite Feet... In the Dojo... Grading defense "Uke No Gogensoku" Shito-Ryu's Special Five Principles of Defense DEMONSTRATED BY GENZO IWATA SENSEI, 7TH DAN JKF SHITO-KAI ASSISTED BY MURAYAMA ALONSO SENSEI, 4TH DAN JKF SHITO-KAI AND NAKAYAMA SENSEI RAKKA (Flower Falling Down) Concept of striking at the base of a tree with such tremendous force to knock the flowers (blossoms) off the branches. Hard blocking applying sudden maximum power with a twisting action executed to literally stop the opponent's attack and destroy his confidence.
