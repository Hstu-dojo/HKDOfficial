# Ura Tsuki — Inverted Punch

**Reading:** *Oo-ra Tsoo-ki*

## Description

The **inverted (palm-up) close punch** — the fist is delivered with the **palm facing upward** at the moment of impact. "Ura" means reverse or underside. This is a short-range technique used when very close to the opponent.

## Technique

**Execution:**
- Deliver the punch with the **palm facing upward** (supinated position)
- Unlike the standard punch where the fist rotates to palm-down, Ura Tsuki maintains the palm-up position
- The fist travels in a straight horizontal line, usually at chudan (mid-body) level
- Most effective at very close range (in-fighting distance)
- The elbow may be bent more than in a full extension punch

**Contact Area:** Seiken (fore-knuckles), fist in palm-up position

## Application

- Used at very close quarters
- Targets the solar plexus, ribs, or lower abdomen
- Follows grappling or grabbing situations at close range
- Appears in kata where close-range application (bunkai) is demonstrated
